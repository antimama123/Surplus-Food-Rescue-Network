Who We Are

About Surplus Food Rescue Network

We are a technology-driven compassionate bridge committed to tackling food waste and eradicating hunger across urban and rural communities.

Our Mission & Purpose

Millions of tons of quality, wholesome food are discarded every day from hotels, corporate canteens, and catered events while millions experience food insecurity.

The Surplus Food Rescue Network bridges this gap using real-time dispatch alerts, strict food safety protocols, and certified volunteer networks to ensure excess food nourishes human lives.

Surplus Food Rescue Network

Official Food Safety & Relief Partner

Compassion First

Treating every individual with dignity by delivering fresh, clean, high-quality meals.

Food Hygiene & Safety

Rigorous temperature checking, clean storage containers, and timed expiration tracking.

Environmental Care

Diverting organic waste from landfills to directly combat greenhouse gas methane emissions.

Our Core Services

What We Do

Comprehensive surplus food management solutions for businesses and relief organizations.

On-Demand Food Rescue Logistics

Real-time pickup coordination for restaurants, banquets, and caterers. Our verified volunteer drivers pick up surplus food within 30 minutes of posting.

Corporate & Banquet Partnerships

Dedicated recurring food donation setups for tech parks, university cafeterias, hotel chains, and large wedding venues with zero hassle.

Shelter & NGO Food Distribution

Direct integration with registered orphanages, night shelters, elder care homes, and community kitchens ensuring equitable meal distribution.

ESG & Impact Certification

Comprehensive monthly reporting detailing kilograms of food rescued, CO2 emission reduction, and social impact stats for corporate compliance.
